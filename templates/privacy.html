Privacy & Confidentiality

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

IncentiveIQ is a tool built by UpSurge Baltimore, a program integrated under the Greater Baltimore Committee (GBC). This page summarizes how we handle the information you submit when using this tool.

What We Collect

When you use IncentiveIQ, we collect nonpublic information about your company (such as industry, size, ownership, and location) to match you against business incentive and grant programs.

How We Protect It

Individual company data you submit is treated as confidential and is not disclosed publicly. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect it against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

How We Use It

We use submitted information to generate your matches, and may also include it in aggregated, anonymized insights about regional industry trends, workforce needs, capital formation, and economic performance. We do not attempt to re-identify any company from anonymized data, and we will not name your company in a public report or presentation without your permission.

Who We May Share It With

We may share individual company data with GBC's cross-sector partners (universities, hospitals, nonprofits, economic development organizations), partners in the Baltimore region's federally-designated Tech Hub consortium, and research or data-sharing partners operating under confidentiality obligations. All such partners are required to keep your data confidential and use it only for the purposes described here.

Your Rights

You may request deletion or correction of your data at any time by contacting us below.
