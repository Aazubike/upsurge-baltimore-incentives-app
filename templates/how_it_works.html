Methodology

How we match and rank programs

This tool never guesses at eligibility silently. Here's exactly what happens between your answers and the results you see.

Where the data comes from

Incentive program data on UpSurge Baltimore is sourced from the Maryland Community Compass, a neighborhood development platform created by the Maryland State Innovation Team in the Office of Governor Wes Moore that centralizes data insights, funding resources, and support to foster thriving businesses and communities statewide. UpSurge Baltimore provides company-profile matching and scoring on top of the State's dataset.

Step 1: Hard eligibility filter

Before any AI is involved, a deterministic rules engine checks your region, business stage, and any explicit industry exclusions against every program's stated requirements. If a program flatly doesn't apply to your region or stage, it's removed here. No AI judgment call, just a direct rule.

Step 2: Weighted fit scoring

Everything that survives the hard filter gets scored by Gemini against a fixed rubric:

Location fit20%
Business stage fit20%
Size (employees/revenue)15%
Industry fit15%
Ownership / MWBE fit10%
Overall program relevance20%

Some programs (from the Maryland Community Compass dataset) don't have data for stage, size, or ownership at all. When that's the case, that dimension is left out of the score entirely rather than penalizing the program for missing data.

Step 3: Match tiers

Only two tiers are ever shown to you. Anything scoring below 75% is left out entirely rather than cluttering your results with weak fits:

0%75%90%100%
Match90%+ fit, shown first Possible Match75–89% fit, shown on later pages

Below 75%, a program isn't shown at all. We'd rather give you a short, confident list than a long one padded with weak fits.

Step 4: Enterprise Zone and Opportunity Zone programs

Zone programs are only shown when your street address is confirmed to be inside the zone. Enterprise Zones are checked against the State's official zone boundaries, and Opportunity Zones against the list of eligible census tracts. Confirmed programs are marked Zone verified.

If you enter only a ZIP code, Enterprise Zone programs are matched by ZIP instead. That's a strong signal but not a confirmation, so confirm your address with the program. With no address or ZIP, zone programs are left out.

Step 5: Baltimore-area vs. statewide

Programs that specifically name a Baltimore-region county are shown first, since they're the most locally targeted. Statewide Maryland programs you also qualify for are shown in a second section below. They are still eligible, just broader in scope.
