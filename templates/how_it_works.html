How we match and rank programs

This tool never guesses at eligibility silently. Here's exactly what happens between your answers and the results you see.

Step 1 — Hard eligibility filter

Before any AI is involved, a deterministic rules engine checks your region, business stage, and any explicit industry exclusions against every program's stated requirements. If a program flatly doesn't apply to your region or stage, it's removed here — no AI judgment call, just a direct rule.

Step 2 — Weighted fit scoring

Everything that survives the hard filter gets scored by Gemini against a fixed rubric:

Location fit20%
Business stage fit20%
Size (employees/revenue)15%
Industry fit15%
Ownership / MWBE fit10%
Overall program relevance20%

Some programs (from the Maryland Business Compass dataset) don't have data for stage, size, or ownership at all. When that's the case, that dimension is left out of the score entirely rather than penalizing the program for missing data.

Step 3 — Match tiers

Scores are grouped into tiers so you can scan results quickly:

Strong Match75%+ fit Good Match50–74% fit Possible MatchBelow 50% fit

Step 4 — "Verification needed" programs

If a program states a specific requirement your profile clearly doesn't meet (for example, an employee cap you exceed), we don't force a percentage onto it. Instead it's shown separately with the exact reason, so you can judge for yourself rather than trust a number we're not confident in.

Step 5 — Baltimore-area vs. statewide

Programs that specifically name a Baltimore-region county are shown first, since they're the most locally targeted. Statewide Maryland programs you also qualify for are shown in a second section below — still eligible, just broader in scope.
